BabyFlow

A simple baby day predictor that recalculates the rest of the day when you enter actual nap times. Timeline shows naps and feeds in chronological order.

Baby template

Set your usual defaults here. The first nap has its own length, and Nap 3 is treated as the long afternoon nap target.

Today's actuals

Leave fields blank if you want them projected. If you enter an actual start or end time, the rest of the day recalculates from there.
Changes also auto-save on your device in supported browsers.
